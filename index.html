        power save
        
        
        
        
      
    
      
    
        power_save_control (+0x09). Bits: touch, motion, haptic, audio, mic mute,
        speaker mute, headphone mute, haptic mute. Power-save mode enables touch,
        motion, haptic and mic mute, and turns off the lightbar/LEDs; the audio
        output path stays enabled. If one stops the motion sensors, the gyro/
        accelerometer above will go flat.
